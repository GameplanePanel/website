## Read player status

Online is live capacity; whitelist and ban totals are persistent access state.

- Compare unusual counts with console and game logs. A disconnect flood (players vanishing between heartbeats) or a ban/kick in progress may explain mismatches.
- Do not treat whitelist or ban totals as current connections. These are *access* flags, not *presence* — a banned player offline still shows in the count.
- Resolve the exact identity before applying an access change. Display names may be ambiguous across games and identity modes (UUID, username, IP, SteamID, etc.).

## Manage whitelist and bans

Each moderation action should be deliberate, auditable, and verified by the game.

- Whitelist only when enforcement is enabled or intentionally staged. Removing whitelist access may not disconnect an online player — the game decides whether to eject them on next join.
- Add or remove a ban, then verify the command succeeded. The dashboard shows the RCON response; consult game logs if the outcome is unclear.
- Kick is immediate; ban is persistent. Use kick to remove a player now; use ban to prevent their return.

## Coordinate disruptive actions

Broadcast and save before maintenance, then verify player count, reconnect behavior, access totals, and audit history.

- Before restarting or suspending the server, save the world (if the game allows) and broadcast a shutdown message via console or in-game chat.
- Test the reconnect path: after resuming, watch for players returning and verify their access is honored (whitelist enforcement, ban rejection).
- Audit all moderation changes in the Console & Events log. Cross-check against the game's own `/list` and `/banlist` to confirm sync.

## Capabilities and game support

Moderation endpoints (kick, ban, unban, whitelist) are available only if the template declares them via `capabilities.players`. Most Minecraft variants (vanilla, Paper, Spigot, Forge) and Source-engine servers support all four; some games support subsets or none.

When a capability is unavailable, the dashboard reports it in the Capabilities row and returns HTTP 501 Not Implemented if you attempt to use it.
