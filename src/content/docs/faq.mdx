Common questions before you deploy Gameplane to your cluster.

        </span>

      </summary>

      </div>

          Is Gameplane production-ready?
        </span>
        <Icon name="chevron-up" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" />
      </summary>
      <div class="border-t border-separator px-4 pb-4 pt-3 text-[14px] leading-normal text-muted-foreground">
        It's in beta ({VERSION}). Core workflows are feature-complete and tested, but not yet
        recommended for unattended production — see the <a href="./roadmap/">Roadmap</a>.
      </div>
    </details>

          Is it free?
        </span>
        <Icon name="chevron-up" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" />
      </summary>
      <div class="border-t border-separator px-4 pb-4 pt-3 text-[14px] leading-normal text-muted-foreground">
        Yes. Gameplane is open-source under AGPL-3.0-or-later, with no paid tiers.
      </div>
    </details>
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col gap-6">
    <details class="group rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card">
      <summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-3 p-4 font-primary text-[14px] font-medium text-foreground">
        <span class="flex items-center gap-3">
          <Icon name="circle-help" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground" />
          What happens to my data if I uninstall?
        </span>
        <Icon name="chevron-up" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" />
      </summary>
      <div class="border-t border-separator px-4 pb-4 pt-3 text-[14px] leading-normal text-muted-foreground">
        Depends on your cluster's StorageClass reclaim policy — Gameplane doesn't force-delete PVCs
        itself. Back up critical data before uninstalling.
      </div>
    </details>
    <details class="group rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card">
      <summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-3 p-4 font-primary text-[14px] font-medium text-foreground">
        <span class="flex items-center gap-3">
          <Icon name="circle-help" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground" />
          What database does it use?
        </span>
        <Icon name="chevron-up" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" />
      </summary>
      <div class="border-t border-separator px-4 pb-4 pt-3 text-[14px] leading-normal text-muted-foreground">
        SQLite by default and is the production-tested driver. A PostgreSQL driver exists but is experimental (build-tag-gated, work in progress).
      </div>
    </details>
    <details class="group rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card">
      <summary class="flex cursor-pointer list-none items-center justify-between gap-3 p-4 font-primary text-[14px] font-medium text-foreground">
        <span class="flex items-center gap-3">
          <Icon name="circle-help" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground" />
          Can I manage servers without the dashboard?
        </span>
        <Icon name="chevron-up" class="h-4 w-4 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" />
      </summary>
      <div class="border-t border-separator px-4 pb-4 pt-3 text-[14px] leading-normal text-muted-foreground">
        Yes. Advanced users can bypass the API entirely and manage everything with kubectl apply —
        the operator is authoritative, so both stay in sync.
      </div>
    </details>
  </div>
</div>
